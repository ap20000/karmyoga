Our vision

Karmayog Foundation’s core focus is to uplift the living standards of the underprivileged and marginalized community.

Through various focus-based programs especially designed to address the needs of the target community in the select project area in rural Nepal. In addition, Animal Welfare and Rapid Response to Natural Calamities form a priority on a need-to-need basis.

Success Stories

School Infrastructure
Strengthening

Most of the government schools in remote areas, where Karmayog Foundation focuses its activities, lack essential facilities: dilapidated buildings, shortages of classrooms, poor sanitation facilities, playgrounds, and more.

We intervene and provide assistance to create a better learning environment. Our assistance is provided upon consultation with School Head Masters, School Management Committees, and Local Leaders to ensure sustainability and avoid duplicity.

Sustainable Play

Interactive Playscape
Project

In the heart of Nepal, we are weaving a story of transformation through our groundbreaking Interactive Playscape. Play is not just a pastime; it’s the key to unlocking the immense potential within every child.

Embracing the philosophy of repurposing, we breathe new life into discarded tires, metals, plastic, pipes, and rubber, proudly showcasing the spirit of "Made in Nepal." These materials, once considered waste, now form safe, colorful, and imaginative spaces.

With your support, we’re not just building playgrounds; we’re reshaping the very essence of schools. Join us in rewriting the narrative for over 35% of children who have never stepped into a classroom.
