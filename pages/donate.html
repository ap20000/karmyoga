Our Vision

Karmayog Foundation's core focus is to uplift the living standards of the underprivileged and marginalized community through various focus based programs especially designed to address the needs of the target community in the select project area in rural Nepal.

In addition, Animal Welfare and Rapid Response to Natural Calamities shall also form a priority on need-to-need basis.
