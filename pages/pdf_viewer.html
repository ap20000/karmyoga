Newsletter Viewer

To display PDFs here, upload your newsletter files to assets/newsletters/ and connect the paths below.

1
Upload your PDF files to the folder assets/newsletters/ in your project. Name them clearly, e.g. feb-2024.pdf.
2
Open pdf_viewer.html and find the PDF_MAP object near the bottom. Add your issue like:
'Feb-2024': '../assets/newsletters/feb-2024.pdf'
3
That's it! The viewer will automatically load the correct PDF when a reader clicks any issue link.
