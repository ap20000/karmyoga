Our Commitment

A Purpose-Driven Journey

"Selfless Service to the Helpless"

Embark on a purpose-driven journey with Karmayog Foundation, where our resolute commitment serves as the cornerstone of transformative change. Founded by the visionary Mr. Saurav Rimal, our organization is dedicated to the holistic upliftment of underprivileged communities in rural Nepal.

Through meticulously designed and impactful initiatives, we address a spectrum of vital areas, including School Infrastructure, Education, Health, Humane Education, Environment Protection, Income Generation, Skill Development, Animal Welfare, and Disaster Relief.

Join the Meaningful Journey

Guided by a mission to create lasting positive change, each of these programs is a testament to our unwavering dedication. Your invaluable support, whether as a Lifetime Member or General Member, plays a pivotal role in propelling these initiatives forward.

Together, let us forge a path towards empowerment and upliftment, fostering positive change in the communities we serve.
