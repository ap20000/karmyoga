Focus Areas

What we care for

Comprehensive, holistic solutions addressing the systemic root causes of poverty, inequality, and lack of opportunity.

🏫

School Infrastructure Strengthening

Most of the government schools in remote areas lack basic facilities i.e. Dilapidated Buildings, Shortage of Classrooms, Poor Sanitation Facility, Playground etc. Karmayog Foundation plans to intervene and provide assistance to create better learning environment, upon consultation with the School Head Master and Local Leaders to ensure sustainability.

🎓

Education, Sports & Extra-curricular

An ongoing program providing school children with Uniforms, Warm Clothes, Shoes, and Materials. We envisage raising education standards through:

• Teachers’ Training Programs
• Setting-up E-Libraries
• Creating Sports Facilities

❤️

Health, Hygiene & Nutrition

We conduct regular health camps by volunteer professionals to monitor the health of children and communities, distributing medicines and sanitary products. To combat lack of proper nutritional intake, we distribute appropriate ‘nutritious fruit saplings’ to supplement diets and raise income.

🤝

Humane Education

The fragmentation of the joint family system has deprived children the learning of societal values and co-existence. The Foundation has plans to develop training materials in consultation with experts, targeted to students in urban areas including Kathmandu.

🏨

Skill Development Center

Working on a Concept Proposal to establish a Hospitality Training Center in the mountain of Chepang Village to serve Nepal's growing tourism industry. Affliated with institutions like NATHM to provide accreditation, giving trainees paths to immediate employment.

🌲

Environment Protection

Encouraging communities to plant trees to protect the environment and raise income levels. We distribute tree saplings suitable to typography that control soil erosion and help water retention, guided by forestry experts.

💡

Income Generation Activities

We work on income generating activities like Handicraft Promotion, Soap and Candle Production. Training Workshops advance existing skills.

Trainings on Sweater Weaving, Sewing etc. are imparted to women. The Foundation provides seed money as a loan, buys back products with a reasonable profit, and sells them in urban centers to ensure sustainable empowerment.

🐕

Animal Welfare

An ongoing program fueled by generous contributors celebrating special occasions. Long-term, the Foundation has plans to establish a "Canine Shelter and Rehabilitation Center" to provide a permanent safe haven for street animals.

By investing in children, together, we have the opportunity to create real, long-lasting change for families and communities around the world.
