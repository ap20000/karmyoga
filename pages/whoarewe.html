Welcome to Karmayog Foundation

We are a worldwide ministry in which millions of children reap the benefits of one man’s clear vision. Our unwavering vision is dedicated to uplifting the lives of underprivileged and marginalized communities in the picturesque rural landscapes of Nepal.

Through meticulously tailored programs, we embark on a transformative journey, strengthening school infrastructure, nurturing education, fostering sports development, and encouraging a spectrum of extra-curricular activities. Our commitment extends beyond these realms. We passionately promote health, hygiene, and nutritional awareness, emphasizing essential sanitary practices. Embracing the values of humane education and environmental protection, we foster a holistic approach to community development.

Moreover, our initiatives encompass income generation activities and comprehensive skill development training, empowering individuals to chart their course towards self-sufficiency. A distinctive facet of Karmayog Foundation is our active engagement in animal welfare initiatives. We recognize the profound bond between humans and animals, and our efforts strive to ensure their well-being.

In times of adversity, our foundation stands as a beacon of hope. With a rapid response mechanism, we earnestly address the aftermath of natural calamities such as earthquakes, landslides, floods, and other disasters. Our dedicated team mobilizes swiftly to provide crucial support, demonstrating our unwavering commitment to alleviating immediate needs.

The Origin Story

Our
Background

How one man's vision during the COVID-19 pandemic birthed an entire movement.

01 // THE SPARK

Mr. Saurav Rimal, an alumnus of Stratford University, USA and an entrepreneur in IT solutions, was left dumbstruck when the Covid lockdown forced everything shut and all movements were restricted. Recovering himself from covid infection, he was at a dilemma and felt totally lost as he had no idea where to begin.

02 // ACTION TAKEN

Meanwhile, the news about the shortages of essentials required to fight the pandemic started pouring in which gave him a push to contribute something to the society during these hard times. Saurav, together with his friends and in close association with the Nepal Traffic Police, started formulating Hand Sanitizers which was distributed to Hospitals, Clinics, Essential Workers and Households.

03 // ANIMAL WELFARE

While his movement was made possible by the Traffic Police, he witnessed the plight of the Street Dogs, Cows and Monkeys, that largely fed on human kindness and leftovers, were totally neglected leaving them hungry and sick. Saurav, then used his own savings to feed these animals on a daily basis. His endeavor was well appreciated and other friends and acquaintances started contributing to this cause and by the time the lockdowns were lifted, he was feeding approximately 300 dogs, 200 monkeys, and 50 cows on a daily basis.

04 // COMMUNITY RELIEF

Saurav also witnessed the difficulties faced by the daily wage earners, beggars and marginalized community living in Kathmandu. Bereft of their wages, they were in dire straits. He started distributing food, medicines, blankets and warm clothes to the most deserved reaching out to over 5000 families. Then the flood and landslides occurred in many parts of the country. He, with the help from his friends and well wishers, distributed relief materials to more than 2000 affected families, accompanied by a group of medical professionals.

05 // THE FOUNDATION

Saurav received a lot of accolades, citations, awards and media exposure for his work during and post pandemic. He was also recently conferred with the prestigious Presidential "Samaj Sewa Ratna" medal in recognition for his outstanding and relentless service to the society. This is when he realized that he now needs to take his initiatives to the next level which is how Karmayog Foundation was founded.
