Chapter 01
The Spark

Mr. Saurav Rimal, an alumnus of Stratford University USA and an IT entrepreneur, was left dumbstruck when the Covid lockdown forced everything shut. Recovering from his own covid infection, he felt totally lost. He had no idea where to begin, or what he could possibly do.

It was this uncertainty that became the seed of something extraordinary.

2020 · Chapter 01
Chapter 02
Action Taken

News about critical shortages of essentials to fight the pandemic gave him the push he needed. Together with friends and in close association with the Nepal Traffic Police, Saurav started formulating Hand Sanitizers, distributed to Hospitals, Clinics, Essential Workers and Households across Kathmandu.

2020 · Chapter 02
Chapter 03
Animal Welfare

While working with the Traffic Police, he witnessed Street Dogs, Cows and Monkeys — who relied on human kindness for food — completely neglected and going hungry. Saurav used his own savings to feed them daily.

His endeavor was appreciated, others joined in, and by lockdown's end he was feeding 300 dogs, 200 monkeys, and 50 cows every single day.

2020 · Chapter 03
Chapter 04
Community Relief

Saurav witnessed daily wage earners, beggars and marginalized families in Kathmandu bereft of any income. He distributed food, medicines, blankets and warm clothes to over 5,000 families.

When floods and landslides struck, he and a team of medical professionals delivered relief materials to more than 2,000 affected families across Nepal.

2021 · Chapter 04
Chapter 05
The Foundation

Saurav received accolades, citations, awards and media exposure for his pandemic work. He was conferred with the prestigious Presidential "Samaj Sewa Ratna" medal — the highest recognition for outstanding service to society.

This was the moment he realized it was time to formalise everything, and Karmayog Foundation was born.
